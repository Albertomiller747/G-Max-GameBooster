Data Deletion & Retention Policy

Under Google Play's Data Safety Policy, the following data is permanently deleted upon request:

  • Personal Identifiers: Display name, registered email address, avatar photo, and custom banner image.
  • Gameplay Telemetry: Uncapped Gamerscore points, total play minutes, session counts, and 7-day play history.
  • Profile Customization: Equipped gamer tags, bio, date of birth, and custom theme colors.
  • Booster & HUD Configuration: Target FPS cap calibrations, HUD metrics, crosshair overlay choices, and recording preferences.
  • Authentication Credentials: Firebase Authentication records and token mappings.

Retention Period: 0 Days (Zero Retention). TitanBooster does not retain any user data once deletion is confirmed. All database documents are purged directly from Google Cloud Firestore.
